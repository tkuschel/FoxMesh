# FoxMesh Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose & Scope
This Code of Conduct outlines our expectations for everyone participating in **FoxMesh**, including maintainers, contributors, testers, and community members. It applies to all project spaces — including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, commit comments, and any related chat channels or external forums where participants represent the project.

## 2. Our Standards
To keep **FoxMesh** productive, collaborative, and welcoming, we expect everyone to follow these core practices:

* **Communicate clearly and respectfully:** Critique code and ideas, not people. Offer constructive feedback during reviews and accept suggestions gracefully.
* **Assume good intent, verify impact:** Approach technical disagreements with curiosity rather than hostility. If your words or actions negatively affect someone, acknowledge it and adjust.
* **Respect time and boundaries:** Keep issues and pull requests focused, provide clear reproduction steps when reporting bugs, and respect maintainers' offline time.
* **Protect sensitive information:** Do not share private data, API keys, credentials, or confidential security vulnerabilities in public issues or discussions.
* **Be accountable:** Own up to mistakes early so the team can resolve them together without blame.

## 3. Unacceptable Behavior
The following behaviors are strictly prohibited and will result in immediate moderation:

* **Harassment or discrimination:** Insults, derogatory comments, slurs, or exclusion based on race, gender, sexual orientation, age, disability, religion, or background.
* **Intimidation or hostility:** Personal attacks, trolling, public shaming, threats, or sustained disruption of issues, pull requests, and discussions.
* **Privacy violations:** Publishing others' private information (such as personal addresses, emails, or private messages) without explicit permission.
* **Unethical shortcuts:** Plagiarism, intentional sabotage, submitting malicious code, or publicly disclosing unpatched security vulnerabilities instead of reporting them privately.

## 4. Reporting & Enforcement
If you experience or witness behavior that violates these standards, please report it right away:

* **How to report:** Contact the project maintainer (**@tkuschel**) directly via **thomas.kuschel@technikum-wien.at**. All reports are handled confidentially.
* **Zero retaliation:** Anyone who reports an issue in good faith is strictly protected from retaliation, backlash, or exclusion.
* **Consequences:** Depending on the severity of the violation, actions may include a private warning, removal/editing of offending comments or commits, temporary restriction from contributing, or a permanent ban from the repository.
